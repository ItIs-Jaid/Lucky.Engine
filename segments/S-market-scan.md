# S-market-scan — PRE-LOOP

**Purpose** — know the actual market before falling for one listing.

## Do
1. Collect 8-12 live listings meeting the hard filters.
2. Record price, size, commute, and date posted for each.
3. Compute the median price of the set.
4. Flag anything priced far below median as a likely scam or stale ad.

## Gate
Scan size >= 8 and a computed median, not an impression.
