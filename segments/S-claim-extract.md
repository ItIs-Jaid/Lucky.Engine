# S-claim-extract — LOOP BODY

**Purpose** — turn prose into atomic, checkable claims.

## Do
1. Harvest one claim per sentence that asserts a fact, number, or causal link.
2. Split compound claims: one number, one source, one sentence.
3. Drop hedges that carry no information ("many", "some say").
4. Tag each claim: empirical, interpretive, or forecast.

## Gate
Every claim is one sentence, typed, and carries a provisional source id.
